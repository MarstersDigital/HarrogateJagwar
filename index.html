Heritage & Expertise

Factory-Trained Authority, Personable Service

When Minster Jaguar in Knaresborough closed in 2009 due to national network restructuring, Trevor Montgomery co-founded Harrogate Jagwar Specialists to ensure local owners kept direct access to authentic dealership-grade skills.

With Automotive Technician Accreditation (ATA) in advanced diagnostics and decades of hands-on mechanical troubleshooting, Trevor leads a dedicated team where you discuss your car directly with the engineer turning the wrenches.
