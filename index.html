Heritage & Expertise

Factory-Trained Authority, Personable Service

When Minster Jaguar in Knaresborough closed in 2009 due to national network restructuring, Trevor Montgomery co-founded Harrogate Jagwar Specialists to ensure local owners kept access to authentic dealership-grade skills.

While our specialist tooling and heritage are dedicated to Jaguar cars, we are proud to look after generations of customers across Yorkshire — welcoming all makes and models with the exact same precision, care, and honest value.

With Automotive Technician Accreditation (ATA) in advanced diagnostics and decades of hands-on mechanical troubleshooting, Trevor leads a dedicated team providing authentic dealership-grade care with honest, personal service — give us a call, we'd love to help.
