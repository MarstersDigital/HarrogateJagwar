## 1. Project Overview & Multi-Page Architecture

- **Primary Objective:** Build a luxury-tier, multi-page website establishing Harrogate Jagwar as North Yorkshire’s premier independent Jaguar specialist, combining classic preservation craftsmanship with modern diagnostic engineering.
- **Routing Structure:** The application must use distinct routes with full page transitions, discrete metadata, and dedicated layout templates:
  - `/` (Home / Landing Hub)
  - `/services` (Dedicated Services Hub & Capabilities)[cite: 3]
  - `/team` (Meet the Team Page — Trevor, Leyla, Keith, and the Dog)
  - `/gallery` (Dedicated Workshop & Restoration Photo Gallery with Lightbox)
  - `/contact` (Dedicated Contact & Location Hub with Map and Form)
- **Core Conversion Pathways:**
  - **Direct Telephone Dialing:** Tap-to-call phone links in the sticky navigation, footer, and contact hub.
  - **Direct Contact Form:** Dedicated enquiry form dispatching submissions directly to `harrogatejagwar@gmail.com`.

## 2. Brand Identity & Design Tokens

### 2.1 Color Palette
- **Deep British Racing Green (`#0A2F1D`):** Primary brand base for headers, footers, dark UI cards, and focal containers[cite: 3].
- **Rich Slate Charcoal (`#121815`):** High-contrast neutral dark tone for body backgrounds and utility panels.
- **Metallic Warm Bronze / Brushed Gold (`#C5A059`):** Accent tone for primary CTA buttons, active route indicators, borders, and badges.
- **Parchment / Crisp Off-White (`#F9F8F5`):** Clean surface tone for cards, text contrast panels, and readable areas.

### 2.2 Typography
- **Headlines & Section Titles:** Refined luxury serif (*Playfair Display* or *Cormorant Garamond*) reflecting classic British motoring heritage.
- **Body Text & Technical Specs:** High-legibility neutral sans-serif (*Plus Jakarta Sans* or *Inter*).
