Personnel & Pedigree

The Specialists Behind North Yorkshire’s Jaguars

Factory-trained diagnostic mastery combined with genuine friendliness. Meet Trevor Montgomery, Leyla, Keith, and our workshop mascot.

Trevor Montgomery · Director & Factory Master Technician

Lead Master Technician

Trevor Montgomery

Automotive Technician Accreditation (ATA) Diagnostic Master · Ex-Minster Jaguar

Trevor’s career with the Jaguar marque began over three decades ago within the franchised main-dealer network. Working as a senior diagnostic technician at Minster Jaguar in Knaresborough, he achieved full Factory Jaguar Master Technician status and Automotive Technician Accreditation (ATA) in advanced electronic diagnostics.

Following the dealership closure in 2009 due to national restructuring, Trevor founded Harrogate Jagwar Specialists to preserve high-level technical expertise for Jaguar drivers across Yorkshire without franchised overheads.

Whether deciphering CAN-bus communication faults on a modern F-Pace or tuning the triple SU carburettors on a series E-Type, Trevor brings unmatched depth and direct client communication to every project.
